dos-arch by AnJ Arch-Tech LLC

dos-arch is a web app for architecture and design practices. It keeps a practice's projects, contacts, organizations, notes, and meetings in one workspace, with context from the team's mail and calendars. Users can optionally connect their own Google or Microsoft account so dos-arch can read that account's mail and selected calendars (read-only). The app is at dos-arch.designs-os.com and is operated by AnJ Arch-Tech LLC, a Colorado limited liability company.
